**Research direction.** Forecast next-hour subway entries and nearby bike departures from the station roles in the first public disruption notice. NYC source records inspected include a ten-minute revision changing an express endpoint under the same line and status. MBTA and Bluebikes documentation supports qualifying Boston as a replication candidate. Five development incidents now link 14 archived notice versions to five focal complexes, 28 nearby docks and both observed hourly counts. Four final updates preceded the first complete target hour. A consecutive three-day audit of 134 first notices yielded 5,002 unique station-hours under the seasonal timetable, including 3,627 with both outcomes. Seven of nine internally identified role pairs retained the original incident and both counts at both stations. The February audit recovered the period-correct base timetable and corroborated the winter clock. Of 31 documented different-role pairs, 14 retained both stations and both modal counts. The weekly seasonal forecast averaged 93.10 entries and 3.61 bike departures in absolute error on 32,903 matched development station-hours. A February chronological pilot scored 19,782 later station-hours. On seven complete two-station incidents, names alone yielded MAE of 145.25 subway entries and 5.46 bike departures, versus 144.42 and 5.53 with roles. Exchanged roles scored 140.92 and 5.70. The comparison spans four dates. Across January–April, rules resolved station roles in 877 of 7,273 first notices. On 103 February rule-named station-hours from 63 incidents, adding roles to full text and station identities raised MAE from 187.44 to 192.97 subway entries and from 4.872 to 4.947 bike departures. Only one scored incident retained two stations with disjoint roles. The four-month dated join now has 282,280 unique complex-hours, 194,871 with both outcomes and 190,647 with both weekly lags as well. Nineteen incidents support a comparison of disjoint station roles after joining. In April the weekly baseline had lower MAE than every tested ridge arm in both modes. On 219 rule-named station-hours it averaged 127.55 entries and 9.868 bike departures, compared with 213.90 and 12.700 for text, names and roles. A seasonal-residual boosted tree evaluated after the ridge comparison reached 80.963 entries and 7.388 departures network-wide, versus 80.882 and 7.454 for weekly seasonal. Adding explicit station roles changed no saved April tree prediction. Next: benchmark extraction on fixed notices without outcomes and freeze the May validation comparison.
