# Text review of possible station-role pairs

On November 28, before opening the selected notices for this review, take the first 32 of the 166 notices with two or more eligible complexes, ordered by the ascending SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string `may-role-support-review-v1/<event_id>`. Use event ID to break ties. Include previously audited notices if selected. Review first-version header and description, eligible station identities, affected routes and the frozen nine labels. Withhold demand magnitudes, forecast values, extraction outputs, and subsequent versions.

Record the explicit roles at each eligible complex, using the definitions and exclusion rules in the frozen example prompt. Include a pair only if two retained complexes have nonempty, disjoint role sets. Segment endpoints share the same boundary role; a difference in direction or an additional role at only one endpoint does not by itself make two overlapping role sets disjoint. Exclude incidental references to an investigation, passenger incidents, destinations used only for direction, unrelated planned-work reminders and restored normal service. Mark ambiguity when the notice or station identity prevents a firm classification, and report those cases separately. Use the operational name in context when a dictionary substring also matches another station. Document the reason for each decision and the evidence clause for any disjoint pair.

This is an exploratory, internal, single-reader audit of development text. It measures how often distinct instructions occur among the 166 name-eligible notices. It is separate from the registered May evaluation. Keep the frozen prompt, scoring rows, comparisons and training recipe fixed. No reviewed assignments enter the forecaster.
