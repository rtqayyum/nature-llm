# Held-out test of selective GPT-2 transfer

The question is whether training a persistence-anchored GPT-2 model for a verification margin improves deployed forecasts and evidence use when the decision rule is held fixed. The original sequential decision rules accepted zero candidates on the two development domains in collection 3100357. In Beijing the empirical rule sometimes helped and sometimes harmed. The choice of a two-thirds look arose after examining four Beijing windows. This commit freezes that choice before these six domains receive new method scores. Their local and ordinary-transfer baseline results were already visible.

## Matched design

Use the original chronological split and the 5% budget on PEMS03, PEMS04, Shanghai, Hangzhou, NYC bike and NYC taxi. For each of seeds 11, 22 and 33 fit the frozen suffix-MAE, label-matched prefix-MAE and verification-margin objectives from the matched pilot. Hold out the full target from source model training and validation. Exclude bike and taxi together and use the same source checkpoint for both. Adapt each target separately on support, with the same local forecast, scale and test origins across objectives. The 45 source fits yield 54 frozen target pairs. Keep every case and report any failures. Exclude development results from held-out aggregates.

## Decision rules and reporting

Apply each rule separately to the same frozen candidates for each objective. Keep the original locked two-sided sequential rule as the primary policy. The prespecified objective comparison is verification margin against label-matched prefix MAE, with suffix MAE as the second ordinary-training control. Report the one-sided mixture and the full-budget empirical rule as policy comparisons. The new empirical rule makes one decision at ceil(2N/3) of the N available chronological verification origins. It uses transfer if mean normalized transfer-minus-local MAE is strictly below -0.01 and at least 12 labels in time have matured. Otherwise deploy local. Count actual verification rows and scalar labels even on fallback. Use the inherited settings for all other rules. An empirical decision carries no confidence-sequence guarantee.

Report all-origin deployed and unconditional MAE, RMSE and physical WMAPE, restricted verification rows and fractions, support and verification label counts, and useful, harmful and materially harmful activations. Include local fallback cases. Average paired seeds within domain, then weight domains equally when reporting relative MAE. Report individual domains, the shared NYC fold, and the paired block uncertainty specified in the pilot. A descriptive comparison counts the two-thirds rule against its full-budget counterpart on accuracy, activation, harm and labels. Fewer labels by construction alone cannot support a claim of improved efficiency. Preserve the original development screen and the Qwen-specific hypotheses as separate analyses. This narrow anchored GPT-2 experiment cannot establish an advantage from using Qwen or reproduce the original paper.

## Execution and independent review

The batch runs preflight, grouped source fits with target-specific adaptation and forecast freezing, and a forecast integrity report. The preflight packages the protocol, policies, objective code and dependencies for S01 review. Candidates use chronological input histories. The batch never invokes a verifier on real target labels or scores the new test forecasts. A subsequent scoring implementation and its exact hash must be included in the independent disposition before formal scoring. Earlier pilot approval paths apply only to their recorded identity.
